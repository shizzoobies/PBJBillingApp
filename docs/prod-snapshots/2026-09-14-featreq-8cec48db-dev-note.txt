Open any draft or reviewed invoice in the month run and the hours review panel sits beside the lines: that client's time entries for the period, grouped by team member, with date, description, task and hours. Each line has a tag (In scope / Out of scope / Ad hoc); picking Ad hoc offers the same three choices as an ad hoc line (Invoice it / Show detail only / Leave off the invoice). It shows the tag a line already carries from time entry or time review, and you can override it here.

Your two answers, built as you gave them: tags stage up and apply together when you Save (the running total updates as you tag, nothing is written until Save, and Print and Send wait until you save or discard); earlier tags are shown and this panel is the final catch.

What Save does with the money: the hours move between the person's Billable hours line and an ad hoc line, at the rate the hours were already billing at, so tagging in scope to ad hoc (Invoice it) is a zero net change and out of scope takes that time off the invoice. One rule keeps this safe: a charge is only added when the matching hours could be taken off the line they were on. Where that is not possible, for example on a line you renamed by hand with no hours on it, the tag still saves (it drives reports and next month's generate) but the invoice amount does not move and the row says so, so you adjust the line yourself. That warning stays on the row after saving until the line is fixed.

Where it is not offered: subscription and annual clients (their invoices have no per-person hours lines, so the KLC combined invoice does not get it), months before June 2026, and invoices already sent or paid. Out of scope also moves those hours out of billable in Client Recap and the firm analytics; to keep hours billable but off this invoice, use Ad hoc then Leave off the invoice.
